# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openalias

**Short description:** Learn why a filmed wheel runs backwards, and recover the true speed: aliasing, Nyquist and FFT, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openalias is a beginner-friendly learning repo about aliasing: what happens when something that changes quickly is only looked at at fixed instants, as a camera does at 24 or 30 frames per second. In three short sessions you go from a spinning wheel that appears slow, frozen or backwards, to the alias formula and the Nyquist limit with a frame-rate design problem, to simulated signals and rendered marker clips whose FFT peak you check against the exact formula, and from which you recover the true frequency by combining two frame rates. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as noise-proof recovery, three clips, multi-blade fans and speeds that change over time.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
